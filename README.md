# Inflation & Pantry Demand Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes the relationship between rising consumer prices and food pantry utilization at the Amherst Survival Center between 2019 and 2025. Using Power BI, the dashboard combines pantry participation data, food distribution statistics and Boston-area Consumer Price Index (CPI) data to explore how economic pressures may influence demand for food assistance.

The analysis demonstrates significant growth in both pantry participation and food distribution over the study period, while also highlighting a positive relationship between inflation and pantry usage.

---

## Business Problem

Food insecurity has become an increasing concern in many communities due to rising living costs and inflation. This project seeks to answer the following question:

**How have increasing consumer prices affected demand for food assistance services at the Amherst Survival Center?**

---

## Data Sources

### Amherst Survival Center

* Monthly pantry participants

* Annual food distribution totals
* Data period: 2019 - 2025

### Boston Consumer Price Index (CPI)

* Boston-Cambridge-Newton CPI
* Monthly observations
* Used as a measure of regional cost-of-living changes

## Key Findings

### Pantry Participation Increased Significantly

* Annual participants increased from 4,746 in FY2020 to 10,716 in FY2025.
* Growth of approximately 126%.

### Food Distribution Expanded Rapidly

* Food distributed increased from 538,384 lbs to over 1.53 million lbs.
* Growth of approximately 184%.

### Inflation and Pantry Demand Show a Positive Relationship

* Periods of rising CPI generally corresponded with increased pantry utilization.
* Scatter plot analysis suggests a positive association between consumer prices and demand for food assistance.

### Growing Dependence on Food Assistance

* Average pantry participants use services approximately six months per year.
* Pantry support accounts for an estimated 25% of annual nutritional intake for many users.
